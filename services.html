How we deploy

From first call to first shift.

  1. Site review

    We visit, assess the risk, and agree posts, hours, and reporting lines.

  2. Selection

    Officers are screened and matched to the site — residential, corporate, or event.

  3. Kit & brief

    Uniform, communications, and site orders issued before the first shift.

  4. Supervise

    Regular supervision, reports, and coordination with the police where required.
