About Mamo Security

Committed to quality service since 2009.

An indigenous Nigerian security company built on discipline, integrity, and people who take their post seriously.

Our story

Built by people who have served.

Mamo Security is a division of Mamo Services International Limited (MASIL), founded in 2009 by Lieutenant Colonel Ishaya Apolos Maitims (Rtd) after 26 years in the Nigerian Army. From a single office we have grown into a company with a presence in Abuja, Lagos, Ibadan, and Gombe.

What has not changed is the approach: screen every operative, kit them properly, train them well, and hold them to a standard clients can see at the gate every day.

Operating policy

Standards behind every deployment.
