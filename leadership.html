Managing Director / CEO

Ishaya Apolos Maitims

Lieutenant Colonel (Rtd)

A graduate of the Nigerian Defence Academy, Kaduna, the University of Lagos, and Lagos State University, Col. Maitims served 26 years in the Nigerian Army as a combatant officer — including appointments as Staff Officer Grade 1 and 2 and Commanding Officer of two units — before establishing Mamo Services International Limited.

He holds an Advanced Diploma in Security Operations and Management, and a Postgraduate Diploma and Master’s degree in International Relations and Strategic Studies. He provides security consultancy and lectures widely on safety and security to institutions and youth programmes.
